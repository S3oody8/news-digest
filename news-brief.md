## Morning digest (8 AM Dubai)

- Window: stories published or materially updated in the last 24 hours.
- Count: 10 to 12 items. Fewer is fine on a slow day. Never pad with filler.
- Mix: at most 3 items from any one section above. Aim for at least 1 from Geopolitics, UAE & Gulf, Tech & AI, and Culture & viral, and 1 Football item when there is real football news.
- Order: most important first.
- No repeats: skip anything already in the "already sent" list unless there is a major new development. If so, the headline must say what is new.

## Breaking alert bar

A story is BREAKING only if ALL three are true:

1. **Fresh:** first reported in the last 90 minutes.
2. **Confirmed:** reported by a wire service (Reuters, AP, AFP, Bloomberg) or an official source (government, company, club), or by at least 2 major outlets. Rumours, "reports say" and single-source social posts do not qualify.
3. **Big:** it matches at least one of these:
   - A war starts, or a major strike or attack with mass casualties, or a strike on a new country.
   - A head of state or government dies, resigns, is removed, or survives an assassination attempt. A coup.
   - A major terror attack anywhere in the GCC, US or Europe.
   - A UAE government decision that affects residents right away (visas, public holidays, remote-work days, airspace or airport closures, severe weather warnings).
   - An airspace closure or security incident in the GCC.
   - A market shock: S&P 500 or Nasdaq moves 3% or more in a day, Nvidia moves 8% or more, Brent oil moves 7% or more.
   - A new frontier AI model release from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or Meta, or major Anthropic company news.
   - A deal or acquisition worth USD 10 billion or more.
   - An officially confirmed transfer of Messi or Ronaldo, or any transfer of EUR 80 million or more.
   - A cyberattack or outage hitting millions of users or a major UAE service.

If nothing clears the bar, return no items. Most checks should return nothing. At most 2 breaking items per check.

## Writing style

Style reference: the Instagram carousel "what just happened around the world in the last 24 hours". One punchy headline per item.

- **Headline:** 12 words or fewer. Punchy, specific, factual. Use real names and numbers. No clickbait, no question headlines, no "You won't believe".
- **Summary:** exactly 3 lines, each 25 words or fewer.
  - Line 1: what happened.
  - Line 2: the key detail or number.
  - Line 3: why it matters, or what happens next.
- Neutral tone. No opinions. No hype words ("shocking", "massive") unless quoting someone.
- English only.

## Sources and accuracy

- Every item must link to a real article you opened, not a search results page.
- Prefer original reporting: wire services, major outlets, official announcements. For UAE: WAM, Gulf News, The National, Khaleej Times. For tech: the company's own blog or a major tech outlet.
- Never invent facts, numbers or quotes. If a detail cannot be confirmed from the article, leave it out.
- Prefer articles with a lead image (it becomes the picture in Telegram).
